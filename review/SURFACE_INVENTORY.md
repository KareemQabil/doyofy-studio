## How this was derived

**Code read (all of it, not sampled):**

- Router and shell: `src/app/App.tsx`, `router.tsx`, `review.tsx`, `data.tsx`, `paths.ts`, `main.tsx`.
- Features: every `src/features/**` file that renders UI, including the Orbit, Bahja and Horizon variants. These were read to the level needed to see where a theme replaces a surface.
- UI: `src/ui/**` (layouts, Overlay, Feedback, Data, Form, Segmented, Button, ThemePicker, JourneyArtwork, ThemeArtwork, Status, brand, `usePointerGesture`) and `src/domain/gestures.ts`.
- Engine: `src/engine/StateMachine.js`, `rsvp.js`, `Quality.js`, `AudioEngine.js`, plus `src/studio/Studio.js`, `markup.js`, `switcher.js` and `src/pages/index.js`.
- Services: `src/services/contracts.ts`, `errors.ts`, `index.ts`, and `mock/store.ts`, `repositories.ts`, `helpers.ts`, `seed.ts` (error codes and messages).
- Legacy entry: `src/main.js`, `src/wizard/Wizard.js` (header only).
- Context documents: `review/STYLE_DOSSIERS.md`, `docs/ROUTES_AND_FLOWS.md`, `docs/PROTOTYPE_STATUS.md`, `docs/HORIZON_REVIEW.md`.
- Test names: `tests/dom/components.tsx`, `tests/unit/*.test.ts`, `tests/e2e.mjs`, `tests/qa.mjs`.

**Screenshots:** taken with the existing `dist/` build (built 10 Oct 11:13) via `node tests/vshot.mjs`, theme `horizon`. Real guest codes came from `window.__doyofy` in a headless page. The images are scratch files outside the project and are not committed; they are cited below as `d/…`, `p/…` and `d2/…`:

- `d/` (1440×900): home, create1, create2, overview, invitation, inv-none, editor, templates, guests, drawer, import, rsvp-unmatched, seating, checkin, gate, settings, summary, notfound.
- `p/` (390×844): home, create1, overview, editor, guests, more, guest-intro, guest-card, guest-hub-yes (`BQKZ9`), guest-hub-no (`UJ65T`), guest-general, guest-unknown (`ZZZZ9`), guest-unpub (`/i/reem-khaled`), gate-party, theme-dialog.
- `d2/` (1440×900): import-review (sample file), tpl-preview, guest-preview, tools, design-review, screen-review, directions, gate-done.

**Abbreviations:**

- **Persona:** O organizer · C coordinator (role switch) · G guest · S gate staff · R reviewer/owner.
- **Responsive:** D desktop · P phone (≤959px for the editor and workspace, ≤900px for dialogs as sheets).
- **Evidence:** `dom:` is a check in `tests/dom/components.tsx` · `unit/x` is `tests/unit/x.test.ts` · `e2e N` is a step in `tests/e2e.mjs` · `qa` is `tests/qa.mjs` (legacy studio states).
- **Status:** "planned — Signature pass" is the planned work. "existing — token pass pending" means the surface only receives Signature tokens and shapes.
- **Grouping:** the event **overview** (workspace home) is grouped under **Home** as `overview.*`.
