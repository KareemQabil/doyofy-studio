Generated by `node tools/asset-briefs.mjs` from one array; `asset-briefs.json` and `asset-briefs.csv` carry the same 37 IDs and statuses.

**Owner rule (10 Oct 2026).** No AI image generation of any kind (no Canva Magic Media / Dream Lab / Magic Design / text-to-image, no AI upscaling). Native vector shapes, the official Doyofy logo and pattern files, Phosphor icons (MIT) and licensed fonts only. No hand-drawn icons.

This replaces the image-generation prompt library suggested in the supplement (§13): every «توقيع» visual is code-native, script-derived from the official identity, or a Phosphor glyph. Open items are Canva-native or code build requests with complete standalone instructions (`request-batches/`).

**Palette and contrast.** Doyofy identity only: gold #C7A661, ivory #F2EAD9, beige #E4D5B4, red #BC4749, turquoise #2EB7B7, charcoal #262626, plus white #FFFFFF and paper #FFFDF8. Text contrast pairs allowed: white on red (5.1:1), charcoal on gold (6.5:1), charcoal on turquoise (6.2:1), gold on charcoal (6.5:1), charcoal on ivory/white. Never gold or turquoise text on white, never white text on gold or turquoise.

**Status totals:** approved-runtime 31 · blocked 1 · brief-ready 3 · existing-reviewed 1 · needs-redesign 1.

**Why one list per mode.** The owner chose a mix of the three explored directions. A, B and C are therefore not abandoned palettes; each one is the visual mode of specific surfaces, so each keeps its own list. Shared identity, stationery and icons are listed once under «توقيع» and inherited by every mode.
